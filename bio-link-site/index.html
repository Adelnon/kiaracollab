Live in three steps

From nothing to a shareable page in about a minute.

  1. Take your handle. Pick a name that's still free — it becomes onelink.to/yourname.
  2. Drop your links. Paste your URLs and the matching icons fill in on their own. Drag to reorder.
  3. Make it yours. Choose a theme, font and effects, add a bio and avatar, then hit publish.

Questions people ask

Is it actually free, or free-for-now?

Free. There's no premium tier to upsell you into — every feature listed here is available to every account.

Then how is it paid for?

Optional supporter perks and low running costs. Nothing that gates a core feature behind a paywall.

Can I use my own domain?

Yes — point a domain you own at your page and it'll serve from there, no extra charge.

How long does setup take?

About a minute. Claim a handle, paste a few links, pick a look, publish.

Do I keep my analytics?

Yes. Views and per-link clicks are tracked from day one and stay yours.
